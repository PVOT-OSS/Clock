Clock is a simple, reliable alarm clock with timers, a stopwatch, world clocks and a bedtime routine.

Alarms you can rely on
• Repeat on chosen days, ring once on a date, or pause a repeating alarm for a holiday
• Labels, your own sounds (pick a file or record one) and vibration
• Slide up to dismiss and down to snooze, or tap if you prefer
• Snooze length, silence after, gradually increasing volume and a sunrise that brightens the screen before the alarm
• Volume buttons, flipping or shaking the phone can snooze or dismiss
• Dismiss an upcoming or snoozed alarm early, or turn off all alarms at once
• Tells you why an alarm was missed, and warns you when battery or permission settings could keep alarms from ringing

Timers and stopwatch
• Run several timers at once, add a minute with one tap, start from 5, 10, 30 or 45 minute presets
• Stopwatch with laps, your fastest and slowest lap highlighted, and sharing of your times
• Live notifications and Quick Settings tiles for the timer and stopwatch

World clocks
• Search more than 300 cities, or add a place of your own in any time zone
• Sort by name or by time zone, and reorder or remove clocks with a long press
• A home clock appears automatically while you travel

Bedtime
• Set a bedtime and days, with a reminder before it
• Link the alarm that wakes you and see how long you will sleep
• Do Not Disturb from bedtime until you wake up
• White, pink and brown noise made on your phone, with a sleep timer
• See tomorrow's first calendar event before you go to bed

And more
• Screen saver with a night mode for dark rooms
• Home screen widgets: analog clock with three faces, digital, stacked, stopwatch and timer
• Light and dark themes

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• No internet permission: your alarms and settings never leave your phone
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/Clock
